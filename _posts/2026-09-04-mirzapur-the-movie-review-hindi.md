Mirzapur ki gaddi OTT se theatre tak aa gaya hai... lekin sawaal ye hai ki **BHAUKAAL** bhi saath aaya hai ya nahi?

`Mirzapur: The Movie` mein Kaleen Bhaiya hain, Guddu Pandit hain, Munna Bhaiya wapas hain... aur Ravi Kishan ek naye khiladi **Babban Babua** ke roop mein entry maar rahe hain.

## Timeline & Story Setup
Sabse pehle ek important baat—ye Season 3 ka continuation nahi hai. Story humein wapas **Season 1 ke timeline** mein le jaati hai, roughly Episode 6 aur 7 ke beech.

Aur isi wajah se makers apna sabse bada trump card wapas la paaye—**Munna Bhaiya**.

## Performance Breakdown
* **Divyenndu as Munna Bhaiya:** Divyenndu ko Munna ke roop mein wapas dekhna nostalgia nahi, proper fan service lagta hai. Wahi arrogance, wahi madness aur wahi dark humour.
* **Pankaj Tripathi as Kaleen Bhaiya:** Unhone ek baar phir prove kiya ki unhe powerful dikhne ke liye chillane ki zarurat nahi hai. Unka silence bhi scene control kar leta hai.
* **Ravi Kishan as Babban Babua:** Film ka asli surprise package. Babban Babua ke character mein woh Mirzapur ke world mein surprisingly natural fit lagte hain.
* **Jitendra Kumar as Bablu Pandit:** Vikrant Massey se comparison naturally hoga, lekin Jitendra Bablu ki simplicity aur calm nature ko apne style mein carry karte hain.

## Action & Technical Aspects
Action raw hai, dialogues mein old Mirzapur flavour hai, aur BGM especially entry aur confrontation scenes ko theatre mein elevate karta hai.

## Major Flaws & Drawbacks
* **3 Hours 17 Minutes Runtime:** Runtime sirf number nahi hai, feel bhi hota hai. Kuch portions stretched hain, second half mein pace drop hota hai aur film ko easily 20–30 minute trim kiya ja sakta tha.
* **Lack of Suspense:** Season 1 timeline hone ki wajah se aapko already pata hai ki major characters ka future kya hai, toh suspense automatically thoda kam ho jaata hai.

## Final Verdict
Mirzapur: The Movie perfect film nahi hai, lekin hardcore fans ke liye ye ek solid big-screen reunion hai. Munna ka madness, Kaleen Bhaiya ka aura, Guddu ka aggression aur Ravi Kishan ka fresh flavour milkar old Mirzapur wali feeling kaafi had tak wapas le aate hain.
